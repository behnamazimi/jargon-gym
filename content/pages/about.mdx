## Why it exists

Every field has its own vocabulary, and every language has words you only half know. Nodding along to them is how conversations and interviews go sideways. Lobyas is built around reading terms often and testing them lightly, so they stick without feeling like homework.

## The name

Lobyas is said **LOB-yas**. "Lobya" sounds like the word for bean in many languages, which is why everyone in the drawings around here looks like one.
